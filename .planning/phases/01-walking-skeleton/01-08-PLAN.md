<objective>
Close review warnings WR-01 and WR-02 in the Dockerfile, then re-prove the whole container with every gap fix from 01-06 and 01-07 inside it (PKG-01).

## Flagged Assumptions (spec-less edge probe)

- PKG-01 / failed install: resolved (explicit). Truth: an unparseable lock fails the build at the `uv sync --locked` step (Task 2).
- PKG-01 / privilege: resolved (explicit). Truths: runs as `app`; a fresh volume at `/app/db` is writable (Task 1).
- Observed while planning, NOT in this gap scope (do not fix here): Docker passes `DB_PATH=` from a `.env` copied from `.env.example` as an empty string, which overrides the image's `ENV DB_PATH`. Under the 01-06 rule the app then falls back to `ROOT_DIR / db / finally.db`, and `ROOT_DIR` is `/` in the image (RESEARCH Pitfall 2), so the path is `/db/finally.db`. That is outside the `/app/db` volume and not writable by `app`. It is harmless in Phase 1 (no database), but Phase 2 (DB-01) or Phase 6 (PKG-02 `--env-file`) must resolve it, for example by making `ROOT_DIR` resolve to `/app` in the image or by keeping `DB_PATH` out of the template. For that reason this plan's container checks pass `SIM_EVENT_PROBABILITY=` and `SIM_SEED=` empty, but not `DB_PATH=`.
- The interrupted-build backstop truth from 01-05 stays with human verification (01-VERIFICATION.md); this plan does not change it.

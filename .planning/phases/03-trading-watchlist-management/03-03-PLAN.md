## Phase Goal

**As a** FinAlly user, **I want to** buy and sell shares and curate my watchlist, **so that** every position stays priced and every change shows immediately.

<objective>
Put trading in the browser: a trade bar with ticker, quantity, Buy and Sell that places market orders, shows the outcome inline, locks while in flight, and moves the header cash and total in the same render through a shared portfolio store that a stale GET can never overwrite.

Purpose: PUI-01, and the browser side of PORT-02/03/04/06. Closes the Header half of 02-REVIEW IN-01. Plan 03-04 adds the positions table on the same store; plan 03-05 reuses `send` and `FormMessage` for the watchlist controls.
Output: `Trade` type, `send`/`postTrade`, `FormMessage`, `TradeBar`, `portfolioStore`, Header on the store, the workspace column, unit tests and `test/trade.spec.ts`.

Tracer: Task 1 is the thinnest live path through every layer this plan touches: trade bar click -> `postTrade` -> real backend fill -> inline success message, proven by host Playwright against the static export; a reload shows the lower cash. Task 2 makes the header follow at once through the store; Task 3 adds client-side input validation and pins every state with unit tests.
</objective>

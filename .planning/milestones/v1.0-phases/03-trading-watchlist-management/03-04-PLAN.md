## Phase Goal

**As a** FinAlly user, **I want to** buy and sell shares and curate my watchlist, **so that** every position stays priced and every change shows immediately.

<objective>
Show what the user owns: a positions table under the trade bar that lists every position from the shared portfolio store and recomputes price, P&L and P&L % live from the SSE stream, with explicit loading, error and empty states.

Purpose: PUI-02, and the visible proof of MKT-08 (a bought unwatched ticker is priced and streams). Plan 03-05's E2E relies on the position row to prove a held ticker keeps streaming after it leaves the watchlist.
Output: `livePosition`, `PositionsTable`, `PositionRow`, the workspace mount, unit tests, and E2E cases in `test/trade.spec.ts`.

Tracer: Task 1 is the thinnest live path through every layer this plan touches: trade bar fill -> `applyTrade` -> `PositionsTable` row -> per-row SSE price -> live P&L, proven by host Playwright buying, streaming and fully selling. Task 2 adds the empty, loading and error states, dimming and every display rule with unit tests.
</objective>
